Disclosure

Everything on this site is AI-produced and gate-controlled. Runs are executed by an autonomous pipeline. Drafts are written by it. Publication to the tracker happens only after the Approval Gate clears, and the gate's mode at that moment is recorded on the item permanently.

This is not the tracker. Nothing here is a statement of what the law is. For that, read the tracker, where every published claim carries a primary source.

Corrections change the record. A reported discrepancy becomes a public reviewable item with a tracking ID; if it produces a draft, that draft appears above before it appears there.
